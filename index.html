About me

Engineer by training, planner by trade, builder by habit.

I studied Electrical Engineering at MIST and did a full-time MBA at IBA, University of Dhaka. Today I work at BRAC Bank as an Associate Manager in Alternate Banking Channels, where I've been the Workforce Manager for the Virtual Banking contact centre since January 2025.

My job is to make sure the right number of people are in the right seats at the right time. That means forecasting how many calls will come in, proposing staffing, building rosters and breaks, and reporting on how the team performs. Along the way I've built Excel, VBA and Access tools that do the repetitive parts automatically.

Before banking I worked as a graphic designer, so I care about how things look as much as how they work. Next, I'm moving deeper into data analytics, data science, system design, digital branding and AI-assisted development.
